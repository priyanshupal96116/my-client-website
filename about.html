Our purpose

GYANORA is a learning website designed to help Class 9 students understand Maths concepts in simple language.

What you can learn

The website offers introductory explanations, everyday examples, practice questions, step-by-step solutions and interactive activities across eight Maths chapters.

Our learning approach

We aim to make learning easier through practice and clear explanations. Students should also follow their teacher's guidance and prescribed textbook.

Important note

GYANORA is an independent learning resource. It is not an official CBSE or NCERT website and does not replace prescribed textbooks.
