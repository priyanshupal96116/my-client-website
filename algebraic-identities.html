Exploring Algebraic Identities

Aaj ka goal: concept ko samajhna, example khud solve karna aur practice se check karna.

1. Easy explanation

Identity ek formula hai jo har allowed value ke liye true hota hai.

Daily-life example: 99² ko (100−1)² ke roop mein likhkar jaldi solve kar sakte hain.

2. Important formula / idea

(a+b)²=a²+2ab+b²; (a−b)²=a²−2ab+b²; (a+b)(a−b)=a²−b²

Formula yaad karne ke saath yeh samjho ki use kab aur kyun karte hain.

3. Step-by-step solved example

Example:

(x+3)²=x²+2×x×3+9=x²+6x+9.

4. INTERACTIVE PRACTICE

Ab tum try karo!

(x+2)² kya hai?
