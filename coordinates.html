Orienting Yourself: The Use of Coordinates

Aaj ka goal: concept ko samajhna, example khud solve karna aur practice se check karna.

1. Easy explanation

Coordinates tell us the exact position of a point on a grid.

Daily-life example: School map: library at (2,3) means 2 units right and 3 units up.

2. Important formula / idea

Distance = √[(x₂−x₁)²+(y₂−y₁)²]

Formula yaad karne ke saath yeh samjho ki use kab aur kyun karte hain.

3. Step-by-step solved example

Example:

Point A(1,2), B(4,6): distance = √[(4−1)²+(6−2)²] = √25 = 5.
