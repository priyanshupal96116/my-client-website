I'm Up and Down, and Round and Round

Aaj ka goal: concept ko samajhna, example khud solve karna aur practice se check karna.

1. Easy explanation

Geometry lines, angles, shapes aur unke relationships ka study hai.

Daily-life example: Clock hand 12 se 6 tak jaaye to half-turn 180° hota hai.

2. Important formula / idea

Straight angle=180°; full turn=360°; circumference=2πr

Formula yaad karne ke saath yeh samjho ki use kab aur kyun karte hain.

3. Step-by-step solved example

Example:

Straight line par 65° aur x hain: x+65°=180°, isliye x=115°.
