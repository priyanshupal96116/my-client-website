I'm Up and Down, and Round and Round

Aaj lines, angles aur shapes ko diagrams ke saath samjhenge aur practice se apni understanding check karenge.

1. Point, line, line segment aur ray

Point: Ek exact position. Line: Dono directions mein infinitely extend hoti hai. Line segment: Do endpoints ke beech ka hissa. Ray: Ek endpoint se shuru hoti hai aur ek direction mein aage badhti hai.

Teen basic shapes

Daily-life example: Ruler par 0 cm aur 10 cm ke beech ka hissa ek line segment jaisa hai.

2. Angles kya hote hain?

Jab do rays ek common endpoint se nikalti hain, to unke beech angle banta hai. Angle ko degrees (°) mein measure karte hain.

Angle ke parts
  • Acute angle: 90° se chhota.
  • Right angle: Exactly 90°.
  • Obtuse angle: 90° se bada, 180° se chhota.
  • Straight angle: Exactly 180°.
  • Complete angle: 360°.
Angle types

3. Angles on a straight line

Adjacent angles on a straight line ka sum = 180°

Solved example

Ek straight line par do adjacent angles x aur 65° hain. x find karo.

  1. x + 65° = 180°
  2. x = 180° − 65°
  3. x = 115°

4. Vertically opposite angles

Vertically opposite angles equal hote hain.

Jab do straight lines cross karti hain, to saamne wale opposite angles equal hote hain.

Solved example

Do lines intersect karti hain. Ek angle 72° hai. Uske vertically opposite angle ka measure kya hoga?

Answer: 72°, kyunki vertically opposite angles equal hote hain.

5. Parallel lines aur transversal

Parallel lines ek hi plane mein hoti hain aur kabhi intersect nahi karti. Ek line jab dono parallel lines ko cross kare, use transversal kehte hain.

Jab parallel lines ko transversal cross karti hai:

  • Corresponding angles equal hote hain.
  • Alternate interior angles equal hote hain.
  • Same-side interior angles ka sum 180° hota hai.

In rules ko use karne se pehle confirm karo ki lines parallel hain.

6. Triangles aur unke angles

Triangle ke teen interior angles ka sum = 180°

Solved example

Triangle ke do angles 50° aur 60° hain. Third angle find karo.

  1. Third angle = 180° − (50° + 60°)
  2. = 180° − 110°
  3. = 70°

Triangle types: equilateral (teen sides equal), isosceles (do sides equal), scalene (koi sides equal nahi); right triangle mein ek angle 90° hota hai.

7. Quadrilaterals

Quadrilateral ek four-sided polygon hai.

  • Square: Chaar equal sides aur chaar right angles.
  • Rectangle: Opposite sides equal aur chaar right angles.
  • Parallelogram: Opposite sides parallel aur equal.
  • Rhombus: Chaar equal sides.

Har quadrilateral ke interior angles ka sum 360° hota hai.

8. Circle: radius, diameter aur circumference

Circle ke centre se boundary tak ki distance radius hoti hai. Centre se guzarte hue boundary ke do points ko join karne wala segment diameter hota hai.

Diameter d = 2r
Circumference C = 2πr = πd

Solved example

Circle ka radius 7 cm hai. Diameter aur circumference find karo. π = 22/7 lo.

  1. Diameter = 2 × 7 = 14 cm
  2. Circumference = 2 × (22/7) × 7
  3. = 44 cm

9. Symmetry

Line symmetry tab hoti hai jab shape ko ek line par fold karne par dono halves match karte hain.

Square mein 4 lines of symmetry hoti hain. Rectangle mein 2 hoti hain.

Daily-life example: Kuch butterflies ke wings mein approximate line symmetry nazar aati hai.

10. Common mistakes

  • Straight angle 180° hota hai, 90° nahi.
  • Vertically opposite angles equal hote hain; adjacent angles zaroori nahi ki equal hon.
  • Triangle ke interior angles ka sum 180° hota hai.
  • Quadrilateral ke interior angles ka sum 360° hota hai.
  • Circumference aur area alag quantities hain. Circumference = 2πr; area = πr².

11. Interactive practice

Pehle khud solve karo, phir answer kholo.

Question 1

Straight line par ek angle 48° hai. Doosra angle?

180° − 48° = 132°.

Question 2

Ek angle 83° hai. Uska vertically opposite angle?

83°, kyunki vertically opposite angles equal hote hain.

Question 3

Triangle ke angles 40° aur 75° hain. Third angle?

180° − 40° − 75° = 65°.

Question 4

Radius 5 cm ho to diameter?

Diameter = 2 × 5 = 10 cm.

Question 5

Quadrilateral ke teen interior angles 80°, 90° aur 100° hain. Fourth angle?

360° − (80° + 90° + 100°) = 90°.

12. Quick revision

Straight angle: 180°

Complete turn: 360°

Triangle angles: Sum 180°

Quadrilateral angles: Sum 360°

Circle diameter: 2r

Circle circumference: 2πr
