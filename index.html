CLASS 6 · MATHEMATICS

Fractions ko aasaan tarike se samjho

Fraction kisi poori cheez ko barabar hisson mein baantne par unmein se liye gaye hisson ko dikhata hai.

½

1. Fraction kya hai?

Socho ek roti ke 2 barabar hisse kiye aur tumne 1 hissa liya. Tumne roti ka 1/2 hissa liya.

Example: 1/2 = aadha.

¾

2. Numerator aur denominator

Fraction 3/4 mein upar wala number 3 numerator hai. Neeche wala number 4 denominator hai.

Numerator batata hai kitne hisse liye. Denominator batata hai kul kitne barabar hisse hain.

+

3. Fractions jodna

Jab denominator same ho, toh numerator ko jod sakte hain.

Example: 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4.

TEST YOUR UNDERSTANDING

Practice Question

Ek pizza ke 5 barabar slices hain. Tum 2 slices khaate ho. Tumne pizza ka kitna hissa khaya?

Answer dekho

Answer: 2/5. Kul 5 barabar slices the aur tumne unmein se 2 khaye. Isliye fraction 2/5 hoga.
