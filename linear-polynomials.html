Introduction to Linear Polynomials

Aaj hum seekhenge polynomial kya hota hai, uski degree kaise nikalte hain aur linear polynomial ko kaise samajhte hain.

1. Polynomial kya hota hai?

Polynomial ek mathematical expression hai jisme numbers aur variables jaise x aur y hote hain. Variables ki powers non-negative whole numbers hoti hain.

3x + 5
  • x = variable
  • 3 = x ka coefficient
  • 5 = constant term
Yaad rakho: x² + 2x + 1 polynomial hai. Lekin 1/x + 2 polynomial nahi hai, kyunki x denominator mein hai.

2. Polynomial ki degree

Ek variable wale non-zero polynomial ki sabse badi power uski degree hoti hai.

4x³ + 2x² − 7x + 6

Iski highest power 3 hai, isliye degree 3 hai.

PolynomialDegreeType
70Constant
3x + 21Linear
x² + 4x + 12Quadratic
x³ − 2x + 53Cubic

3. Linear polynomial kya hai?

Jis non-zero polynomial ki degree 1 hoti hai, use linear polynomial kehte hain.

p(x) = ax + b, where a ≠ 0

Examples: 2x + 3, 5x − 7, −x + 4.

Linear equation ka graph straight line hota hai.

4. Daily-life example

Ek notebook ₹20 ki hai aur delivery charge ₹10 hai. Agar x notebooks khareedo, total cost:

C = 20x + 10

3 notebooks ke liye x = 3.

C = 20 × 3 + 10

C = 60 + 10 = ₹70

Answer: ₹70

5. Polynomial ki value

p(x) = 3x − 2 ke liye p(4) nikaalo.

Step 1: x ki jagah 4 rakho.

p(4) = 3(4) − 2

Step 2: Multiply karo.

p(4) = 12 − 2 = 10
Answer: 10

6. Zero of a polynomial

Jis value par polynomial ka answer 0 aata hai, woh us polynomial ka zero kehlati hai.

p(x) = 2x − 6

2x − 6 = 0
2x = 6
x = 3

Check: p(3) = 6 − 6 = 0.

Isliye 3 iska zero hai.

8. Quick revision

  • Variable: x ya y jaise letter.
  • Coefficient: variable ke saath multiply hone wala number.
  • Degree: highest power.
  • Linear polynomial: degree 1.
  • Zero: woh value jahan polynomial ka answer 0 ho.
