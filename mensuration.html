Measuring Space: Perimeter and Area

Aaj ka goal: concept ko samajhna, example khud solve karna aur practice se check karna.

1. Easy explanation

Perimeter shape ke around total length; area andar ki surface ka measure hai.

Daily-life example: Garden ke around fence ke liye perimeter, grass bichhane ke liye area chahiye.

2. Important formula / idea

Rectangle P=2(l+w), A=lw; square A=s²; triangle A=½bh

Formula yaad karne ke saath yeh samjho ki use kab aur kyun karte hain.

3. Step-by-step solved example

Example:

Rectangle 8m×3m: area=8×3=24m².

4. INTERACTIVE PRACTICE

Ab tum try karo!
