Measuring Space: Perimeter and Area

Aaj hum shapes ki boundary aur andar ki space ko diagrams, real-life examples aur practice se samjhenge.

1. Perimeter aur area kya hain?

Perimeter: Shape ki boundary ki total length.

Area: Shape ke andar ki flat surface kitni jagah cover karti hai.

Rectangle: boundary aur inside area

Daily-life example: Garden ke around fencing ke liye perimeter chahiye; garden mein grass lagane ke liye area.

Perimeter ki units: cm, m. Area ki units: cm², m². Dono ko confuse mat karna.

2. Rectangle ka perimeter aur area

Perimeter = 2(l + w)
Area = l × w

Solved example

Length = 8 m aur width = 3 m.

  1. Perimeter = 2(8 + 3) = 22 m.
  2. Area = 8 × 3 = 24 m².

Answer: Perimeter 22 m, area 24 m².

3. Square ka perimeter aur area

Perimeter = 4s
Area = s² = s × s

Solved example

Square ki side 5 cm hai.

  1. Perimeter = 4 × 5 = 20 cm.
  2. Area = 5 × 5 = 25 cm².

Daily-life example: Square floor tile ke charon edges ki total length perimeter hai; tile ka covered surface area hai.

4. Triangle ka area aur perimeter

Area = ½ × base × height
Perimeter = a + b + c

Solved example

Base = 10 cm aur perpendicular height = 6 cm.

  1. Area = ½ × 10 × 6
  2. = 30 cm².

Height h, base ke perpendicular hoti hai. Slant side ko height mat samajhna.

5. Parallelogram ka area

Area = base × perpendicular height

Solved example

Base = 12 cm, height = 4 cm.

Area = 12 × 4 = 48 cm².

Daily-life example: Parallelogram-shaped signboard ka flat face area nikalne mein yeh formula kaam aata hai.

6. Circle ka circumference aur area

Circumference = 2πr = πd
Area = πr²
Diameter d = 2r

Solved example

Radius = 7 cm. π = 22/7 use karo.

  1. Diameter = 2 × 7 = 14 cm.
  2. Circumference = 2 × 22/7 × 7 = 44 cm.
  3. Area = 22/7 × 7 × 7 = 154 cm².

Circumference boundary ki length hai; area circle ke andar ki surface hai.

7. Trapezium ka area

Area = ½ × (sum of parallel sides) × height

Solved example

Parallel sides 8 cm aur 12 cm hain; height 5 cm hai.

Area = ½ × (8 + 12) × 5 = 50 cm².

8. Units aur conversions

  • 1 m = 100 cm.
  • 1 m² = 10,000 cm².
  • 1 cm² = 100 mm².

Area conversion mein conversion factor ka square lagta hai: 1 m² = 100 cm × 100 cm = 10,000 cm².

9. Common mistakes

  • Area ko square units mein likho: cm² ya m².
  • Perimeter ko normal length units mein likho: cm ya m.
  • Triangle ke area mein ½ lagana mat bhoolo.
  • Circle ka area πr² hai, 2πr nahi.
  • Triangle/parallelogram mein height perpendicular honi chahiye.

11. Quick revision

Rectangle: P = 2(l + w), A = lw

Square: P = 4s, A = s²

Triangle: A = ½bh

Parallelogram: A = bh

Circle: C = 2πr, A = πr²

Trapezium: A = ½(a + b)h
