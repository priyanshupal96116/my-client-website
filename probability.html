The Mathematics of Maybe: Introduction to Probability

Aaj ka goal: concept ko samajhna, example khud solve karna aur practice se check karna.

1. Easy explanation

Probability kisi event ke hone ka chance batati hai. Equally likely outcomes mein favourable ÷ total outcomes.

Daily-life example: Fair coin toss mein Head ke 2 outcomes (Head,Tail) mein se 1 favourable hai.

2. Important formula / idea

P(event)=favourable outcomes/total equally likely outcomes; 0≤P≤1

Formula yaad karne ke saath yeh samjho ki use kab aur kyun karte hain.

3. Step-by-step solved example

Example:

Fair die par even number (2,4,6): 3/6=1/2.
