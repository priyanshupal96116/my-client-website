CLASS 9 MATHS · PROBABILITY

The Mathematics of Maybe: Introduction to Probability

Aaj hum seekhenge kisi event ke hone ka chance kaise samajhte hain. Coin toss, dice aur daily-life examples se concept clear karenge.

1. Probability kya hoti hai?

Probability kisi event ke hone ki possibility ya chance ko batati hai. Jaise coin uchhalne par Head aayega ya Tail, ya dice par 6 aayega ya nahi.

Probability ki value 0 se 1 ke beech hoti hai:

  • 0: Event impossible hai.
  • 1/2: Chance aadha hai, jab dono outcomes equally likely hon.
  • 1: Event certain hai.
Daily-life example: Kal baarish hogi ya nahi, iska chance weather forecast batata hai. Maths mein probability ko numbers se express karte hain.

2. Coin toss ko samjho

Ek fair coin ko ek baar toss karne par do possible outcomes hain: Head (H) aur Tail (T). Dono outcomes equally likely hain.

P(Head) = 1/2
P(Tail) = 1/2

Total outcomes 2 hain aur Head ke favour mein 1 outcome hai. Isliye Head ki probability 1/2 hai.

3. Important formula

Probability =
Favourable outcomes ÷ Total equally likely outcomes

Yaani:

P(E) = n(E) / n(S)
  • E: Woh event jiska chance nikalna hai.
  • n(E): Event ke favour mein outcomes ki sankhya.
  • S: Sample space, yaani saare possible outcomes.
  • n(S): Total possible outcomes ki sankhya.

Yeh formula tab use karo jab saare outcomes equally likely hon.

4. Dice ke saath probability

Ek standard fair dice par 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 aur 6 numbers hote hain. Ek roll mein inmein se koi ek number aata hai.

Example A: Even number aane ki probability

Even numbers hain 2, 4, 6. Isliye favourable outcomes = 3.

P(Even) = 3/6 = 1/2

Example B: Number 3 aane ki probability

Favourable outcome sirf 3 hai. Total outcomes 6 hain.

P(3) = 1/6

Example C: 6 se chhota number aane ki probability

Numbers 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 favourable hain. Total 5 outcomes.

P(Number less than 6) = 5/6

5. Step-by-step solved example

Question:

Ek bag mein 3 red balls aur 2 blue balls hain. Bina dekhe ek ball nikali jaati hai. Red ball milne ki probability kya hai?

Step 1: Total balls = 3 + 2 = 5.

Step 2: Red balls = 3.

Step 3: Formula apply karo.

P(Red) = 3/5

Answer: Red ball milne ki probability 3/5 hai.

6. Impossible aur certain events

Impossible event: Standard dice par 8 aana impossible hai.

P(8) = 0/6 = 0

Certain event: Standard dice par 1 se 6 ke beech number aayega hi.

P(Number from 1 to 6) = 6/6 = 1
Yaad rakho: probability kabhi 0 se kam aur 1 se zyada nahi hoti.

7. Common mistakes

  • Favourable outcomes ko total outcomes se divide karna bhool jaana.
  • Sirf favourable outcomes gin kar total outcomes ignore kar dena.
  • Unequal chances wale outcomes par equally-likely formula bina check kiye use karna.
  • Probability ko 1 se zyada likh dena.

9. Quick revision

  • Probability event ke hone ka chance batati hai.
  • Equally likely outcomes mein P(E) = favourable / total outcomes.
  • Impossible event ki probability 0 hoti hai.
  • Certain event ki probability 1 hoti hai.
  • Har probability 0 aur 1 ke beech hoti hai, dono included.
Study tip: Pehle sample space likho, phir favourable outcomes gino, aur aakhir mein formula apply karo.
