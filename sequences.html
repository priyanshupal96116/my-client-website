CLASS 9 MATHS · SEQUENCES

Predicting What Comes Next: Exploring Sequences and Progressions

Aaj hum number patterns ko samjhenge aur seekhenge ki kisi sequence ka next number kaise find karte hain.

1. Sequence kya hoti hai?

Sequence numbers ki ek ordered list hoti hai. Numbers kisi rule ke according aate hain. Humein rule samajhkar next term nikalni hoti hai.

Daily-life example: Agar tum har hafte ₹50 bachate ho, to total savings 50, 100, 150, 200, 250... ho sakti hain.

2. Sequence ke important words

  • Term: Sequence ka har individual number.
  • First term (a): Sequence ka pehla number.
  • Common difference (d): AP mein consecutive terms ka difference.
  • nth term: Sequence ki n-th position par aane wala number.

Example: 3, 7, 11, 15...

First term a = 3 aur common difference d = 7 − 3 = 4.

3. Arithmetic Progression (AP)

Jab kisi sequence mein har consecutive term ke beech difference same hota hai, use Arithmetic Progression ya AP kehte hain.

AP ke examples:

  • 2, 5, 8, 11, 14... — har baar +3
  • 20, 17, 14, 11... — har baar −3
  • 4, 4, 4, 4... — har baar +0

Common difference nikalne ke liye second term mein se first term subtract karo. Phir check karo ki baaki terms ka difference bhi same hai.

d = second term − first term

4. AP ka nth-term formula

aₙ = a + (n − 1)d

Is formula mein:

  • aₙ: n-th term.
  • a: First term.
  • n: Term ki position.
  • d: Common difference.
Important: Formula mein (n − 1) hota hai, n nahi. First term tak pahunchne ke liye zero jumps chahiye, isliye n-th term ke liye n − 1 jumps hote hain.

5. Step-by-step solved example

Question:

Sequence 2, 5, 8, 11... ki 6th term find karo.

Step 1: First term identify karo.

a = 2

Step 2: Common difference find karo.

d = 5 − 2 = 3

Step 3: Position likho.

n = 6

Step 4: Formula apply karo.

aₙ = a + (n − 1)d
a₆ = 2 + (6 − 1) × 3
a₆ = 2 + 15 = 17

Answer: 6th term = 17.

6. Sequence ko diagram se samjho

Sequence 5, 9, 13, 17, 21... mein har baar 4 add hota hai.

7. AP ka sum: basic idea

Kabhi-kabhi humein sirf next term nahi, balki kai terms ka total bhi nikalna hota hai. Use sum of terms kehte hain.

Example: 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20.

AP ke first n terms ka sum nikalne ka formula hai:

Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n − 1)d]

Formula tab use karo jab AP ke first n terms ka sum poochha gaya ho. Pehle a, d aur n identify karo.

8. Common mistakes

  • Common difference ka sign galat lena.
  • nth-term formula mein (n − 1) ki jagah n use karna.
  • Term ki position aur term ki value ko confuse karna.
  • Har number pattern ko AP maan lena, bina difference check kiye.

10. Quick revision

  • Sequence numbers ki ordered list hoti hai.
  • AP mein common difference same rehta hai.
  • Common difference: d = second term − first term.
  • nth term: aₙ = a + (n − 1)d.
  • First n terms ka sum: Sₙ = n/2 × [2a + (n − 1)d].
Study tip: Formula lagane se pehle first term, common difference aur required term ki position likh lo.
